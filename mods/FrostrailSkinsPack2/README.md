# 人物皮肤 · 扩展二 / Character Skins · Pack 2

给人物皮肤菜单加十三款人物：Ao、AKUMAKO · 万圣节、波覇ありす、SuccubuS、女骑士、kiyria、Ayame Remi · 魔女、エプシロン・ノート、小倉小町、八咫みるく、姫廻ヒメ、羽霞 Waka、めるは。

需要 UE4SS-FrostrailCore 和人物皮肤 0.3.0 或更新版本。装好后打开背包，点「人物皮肤」，新人物排在人物皮肤本包和扩展一的人物后面。用法、背部装备开关和多人说明见人物皮肤本包。只改你自己屏幕上的样子，想换装的人自己装就行。

## 和原模型的差别

十三款都重新绑定到 Frostrail 骨架，制作第一人称网格，并把底色贴图合成图集。下面几款另有改动或特别的样子。

- **姫廻ヒメ**：原模型双手插在衣兜里，世界和背包里保持插兜，持物时物品可能显得悬空。背包预览把武器放在背后。第一人称用重新配色的千駄ヶ谷篠手袖。
- **SuccubuS**：修正了髋和脊椎骨重合引起的拉伸。第一人称隐藏会挡视线的翼和尾，用重新配色的千駄ヶ谷篠手袖；世界和背包里保留翼、尾、袖和爪。
- **AKUMAKO · 万圣节**：去掉两层贴着人物移动的地面法阵，保留帽子、发型和红黑斗篷。
- **Ayame Remi · 魔女**：保留宽帽，裙摆重新配到髋和腿，减少半跪时裙侧被膝盖拉薄。
- **めるは**：重新分配后方两条飘带的权重，减少半跪时后腿穿过飘带。

## English

Adds thirteen characters to the Character Skins menu: Ao, AKUMAKO · Halloween, 波覇ありす, SuccubuS, Female Knight, kiyria, Ayame Remi · Witch, EpsilonNought, Ogura Komachi, Yata Miruku, Himeme Hime, Waka and Meruha.

Requires UE4SS-FrostrailCore and Character Skins 0.3.0 or later. Open the inventory and choose **Skins**; the new characters follow those of the main package and Pack 1. See Character Skins for usage, the back equipment toggle and multiplayer. Only your own screen changes.

All thirteen are rebound to the Frostrail skeleton, with first-person meshes and combined base-color textures. Notable changes and looks:

- **Himeme Hime**: the original model keeps both hands in her pockets, and she does so in the world and the inventory, so held items may look like they float. The inventory preview shows the weapon on her back. First person uses recolored Sendagaya Shino sleeves.
- **SuccubuS**: fixed stretching caused by overlapping hip and spine bones. First person hides the wings and tail that blocked the view and uses recolored Sendagaya Shino sleeves; the world and inventory keep the wings, tail, sleeves and claws.
- **AKUMAKO · Halloween**: two ground magic circles that moved with the character are removed. The hat, hair and red-black cloak are kept.
- **Ayame Remi · Witch**: the wide hat is kept. The skirt is re-weighted to the hips and legs so it stretches less when kneeling.
- **Meruha**: the two rear ribbons are re-weighted so the back leg clips through them less when kneeling.
