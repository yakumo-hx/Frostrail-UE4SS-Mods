# 八云紫娃娃 / Yukari Fumo

在苔原地窖搜刮杂物时，有机会找到八云紫娃娃。地表上抽取通用杂物的刷点和容器也可能出。需要 UE4SS-FrostrailCore。联机时房主和客机都要安装本包。

- 分拣台拆解 1 只需要 15 秒，可得金属锭、精炼金属、油布、线各 24 个。输出格满了就先取走一批，等剩余材料出格。
- 每只热值 999。放入列车的伊甸引擎，按点火即可使用。实际增加的热量受引擎容量限制。
- 拾取后解锁装饰。拿锤子打开建造菜单，在装饰里选“八云紫娃娃”，旋转预览后确认摆放，消耗 1 只。拿锤子对准摆好的娃娃可以搬动。换撬棍对准娃娃，长按“拆解”即可收回 1 只。
- 停用或卸载本包后再读档，背包里、地上和已经摆好的八云紫娃娃都会消失。

Look for a Yukari Fumo among the junk in tundra dungeons. Generic surface junk spawns and containers can also yield one. Requires UE4SS-FrostrailCore. In multiplayer, install the package on both the host and the clients.

- Recycle one doll in 15 seconds for 24 each of Metal Ingot, Refined Metal, Oilcloth and Thread. Collect the first batch when the output slots fill up.
- Each doll has a heat value of 999. Place it in the train’s Eden Engine and ignite it. The engine caps the heat it can store.
- Pick one up to unlock the decoration. Equip a hammer, choose Yukari Fumo under decorations, rotate the preview and place it on the train. Placement costs one doll. Use the hammer to relocate it. Equip a crowbar, aim at the doll and hold the salvage interaction to recover it.
- Loading a save with the package disabled or uninstalled removes dolls from inventories, the ground and the train.
