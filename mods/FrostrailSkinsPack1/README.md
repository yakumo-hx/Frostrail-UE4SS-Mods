# 人物皮肤 · 扩展一 / Character Skins · Pack 1

给人物皮肤菜单加五款人物，由 JustAPal 制作，其中 Celeste 与 SR 共同制作：Celeste、Twf 2.0、Vfg 1.0、Laverisse、Eld 1.0。

需要 UE4SS-FrostrailCore 和人物皮肤 0.3.0 或更新版本。装好后打开背包，点「人物皮肤」，新人物排在人物皮肤本包的人物后面。用法、背部装备开关和多人说明见人物皮肤本包。只改你自己屏幕上的样子，想换装的人自己装就行。

## 和原模型的差别

五款都重新绑定到 Frostrail 骨架，制作第一人称网格，并把底色贴图合成图集。

- **Celeste**：人物移到画面中央，保留细光环、红领结和浅色手袖。
- **Twf 2.0**：人物移到画面中央，帽檐略抬以露出双眼。保留静态魔法书和披风，去掉与之重叠的特效书和手杖。第一人称持武器时不显示书。
- **Vfg 1.0**：去掉静止时漂在人物身外的两组动态装饰和重叠的动画光环，保留尖刺环。
- **Laverisse**：去掉五层盖住底色的描边外壳，恢复外套、泳装边线和耳朵内侧的颜色。
- **Eld 1.0**：原模型的裙尖比鞋底低约 29 厘米，改成逐渐收到鞋底附近，双脚能落地。保留翅膀、鹿角和光环。

## English

Adds five characters by JustAPal (with SR on Celeste) to the Character Skins menu: Celeste, Twf 2.0, Vfg 1.0, Laverisse and Eld 1.0.

Requires UE4SS-FrostrailCore and Character Skins 0.3.0 or later. Open the inventory and choose **Skins**; the new characters follow those of the main package. See Character Skins for usage, the back equipment toggle and multiplayer. Only your own screen changes.

All five are rebound to the Frostrail skeleton, with first-person meshes and combined base-color textures. Changes from the original models:

- **Celeste**: centered in the frame. The thin halo, red bow and light sleeves are kept.
- **Twf 2.0**: centered, with the hat brim raised slightly to show the eyes. The static spellbook and cape are kept; the overlapping effect book and staff are removed. No book in first person while holding a weapon.
- **Vfg 1.0**: two sets of animated ornaments that floated around the body and an overlapping animated halo are removed. The spiked ring is kept.
- **Laverisse**: five outline shells that covered the base colors are removed, so the coat, swimsuit edges and inner ears show their colors.
- **Eld 1.0**: the skirt tips, which sat about 29 cm below the soles, now taper to the feet so both feet touch the ground. Wings, antlers and halo are kept.
